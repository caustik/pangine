# Research tests

This directory contains bounded experiments and characterization oracles. They run in the ordinary test suite so their results remain reproducible, but they do not automatically define Pangine's accepted semantics.

A research test may intentionally capture a known limitation, compare unapproved representations, or use illustrative model constants. Passing means that the experiment still produces its documented result. Promotion into ordinary regression coverage requires a separate semantic decision and removal of the research qualification.

`unified_concept_state.rs` exercises current API behavior around direct root boundaries, structural repetition, finite induction crossover, and generic-match swamping. The corresponding test-only internal prototype lives under `src/engine/research/` because it needs access to matcher internals. Its question-support experiments verify that disjoint source-keyed partitions reduce to the same canonical support Concept as combined state. They also distinguish literal candidate support from a generic three-way path-mass tie and verify that unequal nested policy updates both remain available until correction semantics are explicitly defined, while keeping the proposed numeric evaluator provisional.

`revision_state.rs` compares flat-record, tagged-delivery, and direct-root revision models using the exact recursive Observation closures produced by Pangine's current experience traversal. Direct roots preserve add-only behavior and shared recursive records better than flat tombstones, but the revision markers do not satisfy the stronger distributed question requirement. Independently derived active views do not reduce to the view of merged state, losing a revision partition can reactivate superseded information, and reversing a mistaken correction creates a cycle instead of undoing the link. These are research results, not production state or grammar.

`ordinary_concept_revision.rs` tests immutable revisions represented entirely by ordinary Observations. It records that an observer-bearing fuzzy question is not an exact revision scope: other revisions can still answer, and the old revision becomes the answer when the selected partition is absent. A research-only exact-observer view combines cleanly across partitions, returns no view rather than falling back when the selected revision is absent, and works as the same source view for recursive and global observers. An outer revision Observation can preserve the original source Observation inside a self-contained payload. That snapshot answers without predecessor traversal and shares unchanged interned Concepts, while ordinary union of a base snapshot and delta retains both old and new values.

The short fixture `?[snapshot]:payload` uses the observer position for snapshot identity, but the comparison does not make that a Pangine schema. `?[snapshot-author]:{[snapshot]->payload}` keeps the wrapper's source in the observer position and represents identity with an ordinary Correlation. Both layouts recover the same payload and answer, but their complete roots remain unequal and experiencing either one does not synthesize the other. An application must choose its layout; Pangine does not assign revision meaning to the observer slot or normalize the two forms.

The multi-payload comparison prevents that flexibility from turning into a generic decoder. One ordinary source-observed root contains both a snapshot policy and an attachment, each represented by a complete Observation-state child. Recursive inspection finds both, and they answer the same question differently. Only the application-supplied `[snapshot-v2]` relationship selects the intended child. Exact root membership can return the outer Observation and its direct observed Concept, but Pangine cannot infer a deeper universal payload from opaque names or canonical child order.

A keyed Pangine question is not a shortcut for that exact application step. Asking `{[snapshot-v2]->['payload']}` over the same envelope returns both the intended policy and the unrelated attachment as candidates because `@` retains graded structural matches. Selecting the strongest answer would couple payload access to question scoring. Exact payload access instead inspects the ordinary Correlation and compares its source directly.

The enclosing Relevance remains part of that inspection. One exact payload Correlation can occur directly, inverted, or weighted while retaining the same child identity. The bounded adapter accepts only a default-Relevance edge and rejects the `!` and `x2` forms. Pangine does not infer what those coefficients should mean for an application schema; ignoring them would erase represented structure.

A follow-up reuses `[snapshot-v2]` for two unequal payload Correlations. Source lookup returns both targets, and Pangine preserves their conflicting `cli-runner` and `cargo` answers. The application must reject the duplicate key under its own schema or supply the complete intended Correlation. A Named key alone is not exact identity, and ordinary union does not provide last-write behavior.

The concrete agent-memory fixture carries a complete canonical root into a fresh engine and confirms that `ConceptId` itself is engine-local. Canonical text transports the entire root by value. Parsing it makes the ordinary payload available for inspection, but does not prove that the same outer root belongs to a separate accumulated Observation state. Independently receiving every inner record also does not recreate that outer membership boundary. Reused observer labels expose every matching root, so competing branches remain explicit caller choices. Snapshot choice is also separate from ordinary fuzzy scoring: the chosen full-test policy ranks `cli-runner` first while an unrelated lint record contributes a weaker `cargo` match. Finally, a portable snapshot cannot retain live Percepts. Existing `$` evaluation freezes acyclic values before wrapping, but a Percept cycle remains live.

The caller-contract comparison rejects an add-only in-band `current` Observation because competing choices accumulate and partition loss makes an old choice appear current again. An optional test-only exact-membership view validates canonical root text in an isolated engine because `reference_concept` can execute Percept assignments. It reports malformed, noncanonical, wrong-kind, live-Percept, state-absent, and non-Observation collection inputs separately without mutating the live engine or falling back. It deliberately cannot certify application completeness: a closed delta passes the same gate while omitting the fixture's unchanged lint record.

Exact complete-root membership itself is an idempotent set-membership fold: partition order and replay do not change the result, and removing every partition containing the selected root returns no view. Recursive experience also means that an ordinary wrapper carrying a complete snapshot as a direct default-relevance child makes that exact snapshot root available. This treats the wrapper as transport, but it cannot make availability prove authorization. Conflicting source-specific preference claims remain inspectable ordinary Observations and expose both snapshot roots; Pangine does not decide which claimant to trust.

The caller-chosen root must consequently remain separate from accumulated Observation state. Starting with only an old snapshot, an optional exact-membership check for v2 fails. Experiencing a by-value selection claim then transports the complete v2 root and makes that same check succeed without a separate delivery. The ordinary claim can be useful transport or audit data, but the mutated memory cannot prove that its target was present before the claim. A caller that already holds the complete v2 root does not need the membership check at all. Asking the outer Observation directly does not cross its structural boundary, but unwrapping it with the existing inspection API yields the same payload that state selection later returns. This role separation adds no selection-aware Concept kind or exact-selection API.

The compact-reference comparison finds no interchangeable shortcut for that embedded root. A by-value claim reuses the same interned root inside one engine, but its canonical text includes the complete snapshot. A named label transports no root and can refer equally poorly to two same-label snapshots. A Percept provides local indirection, but its evaluated target changes with mutable engine state and raw experience does not expose either target snapshot. Existing `$` evaluation makes it immutable only by replacing the Percept with the complete by-value root. The caller still owns selection, authorization, and completeness, and the adapter defines neither automatic currentness nor production syntax.

Audit history has the same identity boundary. A label-only `superseded-by` relationship cannot distinguish two complete roots that reuse one label. Embedding both roots by value preserves the exact directed relationship and recursively exposes both snapshots. Forward and reverse audit claims coexist without selecting a winner or changing the payload returned for a caller-selected root. The audit is optional inspectable history, not a correction chain: snapshot questions do not traverse it, and it does not retract the predecessor.

An exact root identifies canonical structure rather than a distinct occurrence. Replaying an identical snapshot from separate partitions yields one structural root, and a by-value audit between two such roots reduces to a self-relationship. If an application needs to distinguish a same-content restoration from its earlier occurrence, it can put a distinct opaque identity or provenance Concept in each ordinary outer Observation. Pangine preserves that structural distinction but does not generate the identity, enforce uniqueness, or infer ordering from its spelling.

A direct by-value claim recursively exposes its nested positive snapshot root, but a claim that contains the snapshot behind inverted or non-default `Relevance` does not. Those claims retain the relevance-wrapped structure and cannot satisfy exact membership for the positive root. They also do not retract a copy received separately. This assigns no rejection, confidence, or correction meaning to the coefficient; it only keeps unequal ordinary Concepts from becoming an implicit revision operation.

The source, matched-context, candidate encoding and its idempotent reduction are structural findings. The finite crossover, equal-source treatment, background, concentration, specificity order, and predictive distributions are assumed evaluator policies. Named Concepts do not acquire numeric, ordinal, missing-value, reliability, independence, or domain meaning from their spelling, so these tests cannot establish those semantics.

The research entrypoint also retains a compact counterexample showing that signed `Relevance::add` is not associative and therefore cannot serve as a partition-independent coefficient.
